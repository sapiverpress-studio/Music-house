How to use this with the book

  1. Read the matching story pages together.
  2. Open that room and use Discover and Hear.
  3. Play the short game once or twice.
  4. Try the real-world practice prompt away from the screen.
  5. Use the interactive Keyboard to hear note names, steps/skips, tones/semitones and the C-major tonic triad.
  6. Use the Music Maker to create with the new idea.

Follow the book’s pace: some ideas, especially keys and key signatures, are introduced for exposure rather than immediate mastery. Keep sessions short and avoid turning the rooms into tests.
